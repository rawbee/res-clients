I wanted to run another agentic AI use case by you that we’ve been thinking about. Today, our ad agency manages Google Ads spend across 147 dealerships. We’ve considered centralizing more of that responsibility within Group 1, but that would require additional headcount.

What we’re wondering is whether agentic AI could fundamentally change that operating model. Rather than building another optimization or recommendation tool, we’re interested in whether an agentic system could actually perform much of the day-to-day Google Ads management currently handled by our ad agency.

For example, could agents continuously:
Manage budgets and pacing across dealerships and campaigns
Analyze search terms and add/remove negative keywords
Manage keywords, match types and other search campaign components
Manage bidding strategies and make appropriate target adjustments
Identify wasted spend and underperforming campaigns
Adjust campaign and ad group structures where appropriate
Manage targeting and other campaign settings
Optimize ads, messaging and creative assets
React to changes in inventory, demand and dealership business conditions
Identify opportunities to reallocate spend across campaigns
Execute routine optimizations directly through the Google Ads API
Measure the downstream impact of those decisions and use the results to inform future actions


The intent would not be to replace Google's auction-level automation. We would want the agentic layer to work with Google's technology while making the broader day-to-day management decisions that Google doesn't have the business context to make.

We would also want to connect the system to our first-party data (CDP) so decisions aren't based solely on Google Ads metrics. Inventory, sales performance, leads, website activity and other business signals could inform what the agents decide to do. At our scale, requiring human approval for every action would defeat much of the purpose. We'd envision defined rules, financial limits and performance guardrails that allow agents to execute routine decisions autonomously while escalating exceptions or higher-risk decisions for human review.

The operating model we're envisioning is fairly simple:
Group 1 sets the strategy, objectives, budgets and guardrails.
The agents perform much of the day-to-day Google Ads management.
Humans manage strategy and exceptions rather than individual campaigns.


Ultimately, the system would continuously observe, decide, execute, measure, and learn.

We've looked at enterprise advertising management platforms such as Skai.io. Those platforms offer significant automation, optimization and cross-channel management capabilities, but we're thinking about this somewhat differently. We're not necessarily looking for another platform that our team would still need to operate. We're interested in whether agents themselves could increasingly become the operating layer. In other words, rather than using AI to make the people managing Google Ads more efficient, we're interested in whether AI can perform much of the day-to-day management itself.

Given the work you're doing with custom agents, do you know of a company that has these capabilities or could help us build something like this? I'd be interested in your perspective on how feasible you think this is as well.
