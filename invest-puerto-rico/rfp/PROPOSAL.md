## C. Technical Approach

*(RFP §5.C — the heart of the 50% criterion)*

### Methodology Overview

We run every systems engagement through the same five-phase framework — **Audit → Normalize → Connect → Codify → Enable** — because the failure mode of CRM projects is skipping a step: integrating on top of dirty data, or training people on workflows nobody wrote down. Each phase below terminates in one of the RFP's named deliverables; nothing is delivered as a conversation.

#### Phase 1 — Audit: Discovery & Data Architecture Review *(Scope: Data Architecture Review & Cleanup)*
- Stakeholder interviews and access provisioning across the CRM and connected platforms *(specific platforms to be disclosed under NDA per RFP §2)*.
- Full audit of CRM properties, custom objects, user views, and existing data-hygiene work.
- Gap analysis: naming conventions, redundant fields, mapping rules, record completeness.
- **Output:** Data Health Review & Remediation Plan.

#### Phase 2 — Normalize: Data Normalization & Remediation
- Execute remaining cleanup: field consolidation, standardized naming, dedupe, mapping-rule enforcement across all records.
- Validation pass with documented before/after metrics.
- **Output:** Data Health Review & Remediation Summary (RFP Deliverable 1).

#### Phase 3 — Connect: Integration Audit & Optimization *(Scope: Marketing Automation, Paid Media, Sales Enablement integrations)*
- Audit property sync, lead scoring, and lifecycle triggers between marketing automation and CRM.
- Optimize ad-platform ↔ CRM mechanics: offline conversion tracking, campaign attribution, automated source mapping.
- Align outreach, scheduling, and third-party tools: activity logging, meeting routing, record creation.
- **Output:** Ecosystem Integration & Workflow Architecture documentation (RFP Deliverable 2).

#### Phase 4 — Codify: Workflow Standardization & Documentation *(Scope: Cross-Platform Workflow Standardization)*
- Standardize lead routing, handoffs, and data-consistency rules across all connected applications.
- Author SOPs and user manuals: data governance, system setups, role guidelines, step-by-step guides for list uploads, lead management, activity tracking, and documentation protocols.
- **Output:** SOPs & User Manuals (RFP Deliverable 3).

#### Phase 5 — Enable: Training, Enablement & Transition to Ongoing Support *(Scope: Training + Operational Maintenance + Troubleshooting)*
- Structured training sessions on CRM fundamentals; onboarding protocol for new users; recorded sessions where applicable.
- Transition into monthly operations: routine admin tasks, permissions, dashboards, bulk updates, custom reports, integration monitoring, troubleshooting, and monthly support logs.
- **Outputs:** User Training & Enablement (RFP Deliverable 4) and Ongoing Operational Support (RFP Deliverable 5).

### Tools, Frameworks & Data Quality Software

We fit tooling to the client's stack rather than imposing our own; the specific platforms in InvestPR's ecosystem will be confirmed under NDA. Our standard kit, spanning both major CRM ecosystems:

- **Data quality & deduplication:** the CRM platform's native dedupe and validation tooling first (e.g., Operations Hub data-quality automation or Salesforce duplicate rules/flows), supplemented by a dedicated bulk data-management tool such as Insycle for large-scale cleanup, and spreadsheet-based validation passes with documented before/after counts.
- **Integration & middleware:** native platform connectors first — they are the most maintainable; managed iPaaS (Zapier, Make, or Workato) where native connectors fall short; and lightweight custom middleware (serverless functions) only where a managed tool cannot meet the requirement, delivered with source, documentation, and runbooks so InvestPR owns it.
- **Monitoring & alerting:** platform-native sync-error surfaces plus scheduled integration health checks with alert routing to email/Slack, so failures announce themselves instead of surfacing weeks later in a broken report.
- **Documentation & training:** SOPs and manuals in InvestPR's preferred workspace (Google Workspace, Notion, or SharePoint); recorded walkthrough videos (Loom) accompanying each training session.
- **Project management:** a shared board (Asana, Linear, or InvestPR's preferred tool) visible to InvestPR at all times — statuses, owners, and dates, never a black box.

### Support Model & SLAs

- **Intake:** a dedicated support email plus a shared Slack (or Teams) channel with both principals; requests are logged into the shared board so nothing lives only in an inbox.
- **Response-time targets:** acknowledgment within 4 business hours for all requests. **Severity 1** (data loss, broken sync, or a down integration affecting daily operations): work begins same business day. **Severity 2** (degraded workflow, reporting errors): response within 1 business day. **Severity 3** (routine admin, report builds, training requests): scheduled within 5 business days.
- **Coverage:** business hours aligned to InvestPR's Atlantic Standard Time workday.
- **Escalation:** both principals are directly reachable; there is no tiered queue between InvestPR and the people who built the system.
- **Monthly support log:** delivered with each invoice — every request, resolution, time spent, and any recurring issue flagged with a root-cause recommendation.
